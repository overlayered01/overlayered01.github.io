Privacy Policy

WordPing (Android package: com.overlayered.vocabexamples)
Effective date: November 7, 2026

WordPing ("the app") is developed by overlayered ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not show ads or use analytics.

1. Information stored on your device

Your words, meanings, example sentences, wordbooks, study history and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive or have access to this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Information sent to external services

To provide dictionary and example features, the app sends the word you look up (not your personal information) to the following services. Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

ServicePurposeData sent
Free Dictionary API (freedictionaryapi.com)DefinitionsSearched word
Tatoeba (api.tatoeba.org)Example sentences and translationsSearched word, selected language
Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org)Pronunciation audioSearched word
GitHub / jsDelivrDownloading translation data packsNone beyond a standard download request

3. Photos and text recognition

If you choose a photo to extract example sentences, text recognition runs on your device using Google ML Kit. The photo and the recognized text are not uploaded to us. Google ML Kit may send anonymous diagnostic and usage information to Google (ML Kit terms).

4. Notifications and speech

Word notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Text-to-speech uses your device's built-in speech engine.

5. External links

The app may open external websites (such as online dictionaries or Google Translate) in your browser. Those sites are governed by their own policies.

6. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if accounts, cloud sync or ads are added), we will update this page and the effective date before the change takes effect.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: overlayered01@gmail.com

사전·예문 기능을 위해 앱은 사용자가 조회한 단어(개인정보 아님)를 아래 서비스로 보냅니다. 일반적인 인터넷 요청과 마찬가지로 해당 서비스는 기기의 IP 주소를 알 수 있으며, 각 서비스의 개인정보처리방침이 적용됩니다.
